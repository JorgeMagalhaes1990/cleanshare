## Current proposition

OutZila is a protected peer-to-peer marketplace for individual premium outdoor and leisure equipment. The initial insured catalogue is limited to identifiable individual items with acquisition or replacement value above EUR 500, practical car-compatible transport and an inspectable condition at collection and return. The platform does not rent vehicles and does not accept multi-item camping, ski or snowboard sets as one insured item.

The paid value is a trusted operation: verified identity, an electronically signed rental contract, validated payment and deposit, embedded insurance, private pre-confirmation chat, documented bilateral collection and return, timed return confirmation and incident handling.

## Launch, pilot and expansion

Portugal is the controlled operational and insurance pilot. The Year 1 target of 1,500 operations relates only to Portugal in the first twelve months after launch. Portugal is not the geographic limit: the intended next priority markets are Spain, France, Germany and Italy. A potential insurance partner must be able to support, or provide a credible route to support, this European expansion.

## Insurance

Embedded insurance is mandatory for each eligible rental from launch. The requested core cover comprises accidental damage, third-party theft or robbery, total loss, and non-return or unlawful appropriation by the renter. Non-return is a launch requirement. A generic theft clause does not demonstrate that non-return is covered.

The renter pays 100% of the insurance premium. The working premium assumption of 10% of rental value is a modelling hypothesis, not an agreed tariff. A potential 10% OutZila distribution remuneration on the premium is also a hypothesis, subject to an insurer agreement and the applicable insurance-distribution and regulatory structure. OutZila does not fund 30% of the premium.

## Identity, signature and evidence

Supabase owns accounts, login and sessions. A specialised European identity and electronic-signature provider supports civil verification and signature; Signicat is preferred, subject to contract and implementation.

Onboarding supports national eID or EUDI Wallet when available, plus an alternative universal path based on identity document, selfie/liveness and NFC when applicable. eID/EUDI is not mandatory at launch. Store only verified attributes necessary for the operation and contract. Avoid storing identity documents and biometric data unless a documented legal requirement demands it.

The evidence chain for every rental is:

`verified identity -> signed contract -> validated payment -> documented collection -> documented return`
