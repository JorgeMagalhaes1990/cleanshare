# CleanShare - Project Context

## What is CleanShare?

CleanShare is a curated, generalist peer-to-peer marketplace for renting high-value equipment that is only needed occasionally.

The platform connects private equipment owners with people who need a specific item for a short period. It is not exclusive to cleaning equipment: the initial catalog covers home projects, deep cleaning, garden and land maintenance, and events and leisure.

---

## Mission

Help people generate extra income from useful equipment that spends most of its time unused.

At the same time, give other people safe, convenient access to equipment without requiring a purchase for a one-off or infrequent task.

---

## Strategic Thesis and Defensible Advantage

CleanShare must not compete with broad classifieds or resale marketplaces on listing volume alone. Its central strategic advantage is the trusted operational layer that makes an equipment rental safe from beginning to end: user authentication, contract formation, payment and deposit handling, before-and-after condition evidence, workflow tracking and conflict resolution.

The defensible asset is the accumulated operational knowledge, rules, evidence model and decision history required to protect both parties. A catalog of listings by itself is easy to reproduce. Product development must keep the user experience simple while the platform manages the necessary complexity internally.

The strategic direction is approved, but its economics remain a hypothesis that the pilot must validate. Revenue per transaction must cover payment and insurance costs, support time, fraud and damage exposure, incident handling and the average cost of resolving conflicts, while leaving a positive contribution margin. If the protection layer cannot be financed, CleanShare must adjust commission, eligible categories or service level before expanding.

Pilot measurements must therefore include average commission, variable payment and insurance cost, support time per transaction, incident and dispute rate, average resolution cost and contribution margin after protection costs.

---

## Marketplace Focus

CleanShare is generalist but curated. It should not try to list every possible object.

Private individuals are the initial source of supply. The first phase is designed to validate both owner supply and renter demand within a focused catalog.

Equipment is preferably eligible when it meets all of these principles:

- Acquisition value above €300.
- Occasional rather than continuous use.
- Simple transport by an ordinary user or vehicle.
- Condition and operation that can be verified through a practical inspection.
- Low or moderate operational and liability risk.

These principles guide curation; the valuation matrix and current database migrations are the operational source for category-level decisions.

## Removable Automotive Leisure Accessories

CleanShare does not rent vehicles. Automotive items in the catalog are removable leisure accessories that are normally stored in private homes or garages and rented independently from any vehicle.

Before an accessory can be used, the operation must verify:

- Compatibility with the renter's vehicle and its fixing points.
- Applicable roof, towbar and accessory load limits.
- Complete, secure mounting in line with the manufacturer's instructions.
- Cleanliness and hygiene between rentals, including verified sanitisation of sleeping bags.

Public content and imagery must not imply that a vehicle is included in the rental. The equipment itself is the marketplace item.

---

## B2B and Industrial Scope

B2B and industrial equipment belong to a later expansion phase, not the initial marketplace offer.

That phase requires its own eligibility, risk, logistics, inspection, support and contractual rules. Public content must not imply that B2B or industrial supply is already part of the launch scope.

## Approved Operating Benchmark: Yescapa

Yescapa is the primary operating-model reference for CleanShare. This is an approved strategic decision: use a proven peer-to-peer rental architecture and adapt it to equipment. It is not permission to copy Yescapa's branding, interface, wording, code, legal terms or insurance arrangements.

The CleanShare product should follow the same underlying transaction logic:

- Curated listing and validation of the owner, equipment and required documents.
- Search, request and private pre-confirmation conversation between the participants.
- Owner acceptance followed by protected payment and deposit handling.
- A contract generated from the accepted reservation and its authoritative snapshots.
- Coordinated collection with identity/document checks, bilateral inventory and photographic evidence.
- A visible operational period with role-specific actions, support information and controlled contact disclosure.
- Coordinated return with a second bilateral inventory, before/after comparison and a strict response deadline.
- Authoritative completion, owner payout and renter deposit release when the return is accepted or the supported deadline expires without a dispute.
- Mutual reviews after completion and platform mediation when the evidence or participants disagree.

The service fee must be presented and designed as payment for the trusted operational layer, not merely for publishing a listing. It must finance the variable costs of payments, deposits, identity/document checks, insurance where applicable, support, fraud handling and incident resolution while preserving positive contribution margin.

CleanShare must not implement one undifferentiated risk model across the catalog. The shared rental state machine remains common, while each equipment category or risk class configures eligibility, deposit policy, insurance requirements, inventory fields, required photographs, exclusions, deadlines, mounting or usage instructions and incident rules.

Product and development implications:

- Owner and renter journeys are parallel views of the same authoritative operation.
- Every dashboard state identifies the next action, the responsible participant and the applicable deadline.
- Chat supports agreement and clarification but never replaces structured confirmations, contracts or evidence.
- Contact details are disclosed only at the operational point where direct coordination is necessary and permitted.
- Handover and return records are completed by both participants and become immutable audit evidence after confirmation.
- Payment, deposit, contract, insurance, reviews and dispute handling belong to one end-to-end product flow, even when introduced over several MVPs.
- A material departure from this benchmark must be justified by equipment characteristics, Portuguese law, provider constraints or measured pilot evidence.

Yescapa remains an external benchmark, not legal authority. Its current flow, fees and coverage may vary by market and over time. Before production, CleanShare must independently validate Portuguese consumer, contract, payment, insurance, tax, privacy and platform-liability requirements with appropriate specialists and regulated providers.

Primary reference pages reviewed in September 2026: https://www.yescapa.pt/, https://www.yescapa.pt/how-yescapa-works, https://www.yescapa.pt/rental-contract, https://www.yescapa.pt/todos-nossos-seguros/, https://www.yescapa.pt/todas-nossas-assistencias/ and https://www.yescapa.pt/ajuda/funcionamento-do-site/a-que-correspondem-os-custos-de-servico/.

## Main Value Proposition

The biggest challenge is not the rental itself. The biggest challenge is making both parties feel safe throughout the transaction.

CleanShare sells confidence.

Everything on the website should reinforce:

- Security.
- Trust.
- Simplicity.
- Professionalism.

---

## Pillars

### Security

Owners must feel that their equipment and payments are protected, and renters must understand the terms of the operation.

Examples:

- Security deposits.
- Identity verification.
- Digital contracts.
- Insurance when applicable.
- Secure payments.
- Confirmed handover and return.

### Trust

Every interaction must increase trust. Avoid anything that looks cheap, amateur or exaggerated. The experience should feel premium and credible.

### Simplicity

The platform must look easy to use. Core actions should require as few steps as possible, while safety conditions remain clear.

## User Area

The personal area is called “Minha CleanShare”. One account can act as both equipment owner and renter; these are roles within the same profile, not separate account types.

The main navigation covers the overview, operations, owned equipment, exploration and rental requests, messages, payments, documents, profile and security, and help. The experience is organised around operations, pending actions and a clear explanation of who must do what next.

The normal route, `/area-utilizador.html`, is the real personal area. It requires a Supabase session, reads the authenticated user's own profile and must never fall back to fictitious content after a session or profile error. The route `/area-utilizador.html?demo=1` is the explicit demonstration mode and preserves labelled example operations, values and interactions.

CMD remains the future identity-verification requirement for operating on CleanShare. During the controlled pilot only, a single reversible configuration may allow authenticated users to test operations without verified CMD. This exception must always be described as temporary “pilot access”, never as verified identity, and must not alter the real `verification_status` shown to the user.

Owner and renter preferences may be stored in `auth.user_metadata` to personalise the interface. They are not authorization claims and must never replace database-backed permissions or RLS in future operational flows. The same limitation applies to notification preferences.

The interface must not imply that payments, deposits, contracts, insurance or other legal and financial actions have been executed when the real integrations are not connected. User autonomy is limited to safe interface previews and supported profile updates; consequential actions require the future verification, transaction and database services.

MVP 3 enables the first real pilot operation between two distinct authenticated accounts: one account publishes an eligible equipment listing and another account requests it. The pilot records and exposes only the operational request, owner response and status transitions supported by the database. It does not process payments, block deposits, create a legally valid contract, activate insurance or require completed CMD while the temporary pilot exception is enabled.

Rental creation, price and deposit snapshots, inclusive-day totals, conflict checks and status transitions are authoritative RPC responsibilities. Clients must never insert rentals directly or provide trusted totals. The participant listing may expose the counterpart's display name because both users already share an operation. Telephone is disclosed only after confirmation and only while the operation is confirmed, in progress, completed or disputed; email and full address remain private.

Participants may use the private operation chat from the requested state and throughout supported operational states. Messages are trimmed, limited to 1000 characters, immutable and visible only to the owner and renter of that rental. Chat must never be used to expose participant contacts before the database permits the dedicated post-confirmation contact field.

Handover and return use bilateral condition reports. Each participant uploads their own private photographic evidence and explicitly confirms only their own observation. The database moves a confirmed rental to `in_progress` only after two handover confirmations. Return should be confirmed immediately by both participants; the first return report starts a maximum 24-hour window for the counterparty. Two return confirmations complete the rental immediately. If the second confirmation is still absent when that window expires, an authenticated participant refresh triggers authoritative automatic completion. Evidence files use participant-scoped private paths and short-lived signed URLs. Confirmed reports and messages are audit records; deleting a rental with those records is deliberately blocked rather than cascaded.

Automatic completion is an operational pilot rule, not a financial claim. During the pilot it changes only the rental state. In production, the payment and deposit services must consume that authoritative completion event so that the owner payment and renter deposit are released without avoidable delay, except when a supported dispute has suspended completion.

Essential operational email notifications use a provider-neutral transactional outbox. The database, not browser code, decides which events create notifications and enforces a permanent unique event key so retries cannot queue duplicates. The supported events are: new request to the owner; accepted, rejected or cancelled request to the waiting party; first handover confirmation to the participant who must still act; first return confirmation with the 24-hour deadline to the participant who must still act; and completion to both participants. Chat messages, photo uploads and routine progress changes do not generate email.

Email payloads stored in the outbox contain only recipient user ID, rental ID, equipment title, dates, role and deadline when required. Email addresses are resolved from Supabase Auth only inside a trusted Edge Function. Messages must remain short, exclude contact details, addresses, photographs and documents, and link back to the authenticated user area. Delivery attempts, provider IDs and terminal failures are auditable; credentials must remain in Edge Function secrets.

Resend is the initial transactional provider because it integrates directly with Supabase Edge Functions, supports authenticated sending domains, delivery events and provider-side idempotency. The dispatcher also includes an Amazon SES adapter for a future EU-region requirement. Templates, event selection, retry state and business idempotency remain owned by CleanShare, so changing providers does not require rebuilding the operational flow. Queueing remains disabled until the sending subdomain, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, provider credentials, webhook, retry schedule and two-account test have all been validated.

Operational email checkpoint (2026-09-02): the Resend provider credentials, Edge Function, authenticated INSERT webhook and one-minute retry schedule are configured in the Supabase project. A controlled sandbox message to the provider account owner was accepted by the Edge Function and reported as delivered by Resend. Global outbox queueing remains disabled. The temporary `onboarding@resend.dev` sender is not suitable for the two-account pilot or production. Once the product name and domain are final, authenticate a dedicated sending subdomain, replace `EMAIL_FROM`, optionally set a monitored `EMAIL_REPLY_TO`, test delivery to both pilot accounts, and only then enable queueing. No provider or dispatch secret may be committed to the repository.

Photographs are supporting evidence, not an expert inspection. This workflow does not change the pilot limits: payment is not processed, deposit is not blocked, contract has no legal validity, insurance is not activated and CMD is not mandatory while the explicit pilot exception is enabled.

The before/after photographic dossier is an internal operation record visible only to the two authenticated rental participants. It must never appear in listings, catalog pages, public profiles or the landing page. Photographs support operational follow-up and potential conflict analysis, but do not decide a disagreement. A future CleanShare support/backoffice flow must intervene when participants disagree; dispute actions, states, promises and resolution automation are outside the current MVP.

Role preferences in user metadata continue to personalise which owner and renter tools are shown. They do not authorize publication, requests or transitions: database ownership checks, authenticated identity, RLS and security-definer RPC validation remain authoritative. Demo mode remains isolated at `?demo=1` and must not call or resemble the real pilot workflow.

## Future Vision

CleanShare should become Portugal's trusted peer-to-peer marketplace for equipment that people need occasionally but do not need to own.

The marketplace can expand into additional curated categories and later into B2B or industrial use cases, while maintaining the same standards of trust, safety, simplicity and professional quality.
