01 · Why rent outdoor equipment?

The ownership mismatch.

Many outdoor products are expensive to buy, used only a few times a year and then stored for long periods.

I

Demand is there

Camping, road trips, cycling and outdoor holidays remain large European travel behaviours.

II

Buying is expensive

Premium gear can cost hundreds or thousands of euros, even for occasional use.

III

Rental is fragmented

Supply exists, but is still scattered across niche operators, classifieds and single-category platforms.

IV

Gear sits idle

Privately owned equipment spends most of its life in garages, storage rooms and sheds.

02 · The problem / opportunity

Owners can monetise idle gear.
But they need to trust the transaction.

RISK 01

Accidental damage

The item is returned, but damaged during transport, installation or use.

RISK 02

Third-party theft

The item is stolen while legitimately in the renter's possession.

RISK 03

Non-return

The renter legitimately receives the item and then fails to return it.

OutZila's job is not just to match two people. It is to make the whole transaction trustworthy.

Appendix A · Trend research

What the data actually supports.

1. Camping / caravanning has grown structurally

Eurostat reports 413 million nights at EU camping grounds, RV parks and trailer parks in 2025. That was 28.5% above 2015, versus +23.4% for hotels, holiday and other short-stay accommodation over the same period.

2. The largest target markets are already deep outdoor-tourism markets

In 2025: France recorded 154m camping/RV nights, Spain 49.8m, Italy 49.1m, Germany 45.0m and Portugal 7.1m.

3. Leisure-vehicle demand is also visible in registrations

ECF data shows European motor-caravan registrations rose 9.5% in 2024. Total leisure-vehicle registrations rose 5.2%, including strong total-market growth in France (+7.6%), Germany (+6.7%), Italy (+17.8%) and Spain (+17.1%).

4. Travel demand remains high, but value matters

ETC reported 82% of Europeans planned to travel in spring/summer 2026, while travellers were becoming more cautious with shorter trips and more moderate budgets. Its summer/autumn 2026 research also highlights affordability as a factor shaping destination choice.

Appendix A · Affordability

Are higher tourism prices pushing people towards lower-cost formats?

Conclusion: the evidence supports a strong affordability pressure in European travel and a search for value. It does not prove that higher hotel/package prices alone are causing the rise in camping or motorhome holidays. For OutZila, affordability should therefore be presented as a supporting tailwind, not the sole explanation for outdoor-tourism growth.

Appendix A · Supply reality

Rental exists — but it is fragmented.

Portugal: visible private inventory

OLX Portugal currently shows more than 100 search results for rooftop tents in Camping and more than 150 for bike carriers in Parts & Accessories. Many rooftop-tent listings are explicitly marked as used. Separate rental searches surface dozens of rooftop-tent and roof-box offers. These figures are directional because live marketplace searches can include adjacent matches, but they show a meaningful installed base and owner willingness to sell or monetise equipment.

Europe: fragmented category-by-category supply

Comparable peer-to-peer platforms already exist, but many remain narrow by geography or product category. OutZila is designed around a broader security layer for the transaction itself: verified identity, validated equipment, platform payments and deposit, private rental contract, documented handover/return and embedded insurance — including non-return protection — across several high-value outdoor categories.

Important: marketplace listings do not prove how many assets are literally sitting unused in garages. They are evidence of existing private ownership, second-hand circulation and a demonstrated willingness by owners to monetise these assets — which supports the underutilised-asset thesis.

Appendix C · Contract & identity logic

Evidence, not paperwork for paperwork's sake.

Rental contract

Transaction-specific: verified parties, exact equipment, dates, price, deposit, insurance reference, initial/final condition, responsibilities, late return, loss/theft/non-return and dispute rules.

Identity strategy

Supabase remains the account/session layer. The preferred European identity architecture supports eID/EUDI Wallet where available and ID document + selfie/liveness/NFC as fallback. Only necessary verified attributes should be retained by OutZila.
