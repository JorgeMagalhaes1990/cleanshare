# OutZila AI Workflow

## Required reading order

Before work that affects strategy, product, design, legal terms, insurance, identity, payment, evidence, operational email or presentations, read:

1. `PROJECT_CONTEXT.txt`
2. `docs/strategy/OutZila_Blueprint_v2.0.md`
3. the specialist document relevant to the task in `docs/strategy`, `docs/insurance` or `docs/legal`

For any commercial or external-facing material, use `docs/presentations/Hi_Im_OutZila_v0.5.html` and the current business plan. This is the current institutional web presentation, published at `https://outzila.com/hi` with noindex. Future edits must build on this version and be versioned in `docs/presentations/`. Do not copy figures from legacy CleanShare or OutShare files.

## Authority rules

- OutZila is the approved brand.
- CleanShare and OutShare content is legacy where it conflicts with current OutZila documents.
- The official launch scope is individual premium outdoor and leisure equipment, normally above EUR 500. Multi-item sets are excluded.
- Portugal is the controlled pilot only. The intended expansion path is Spain, France, Germany and Italy.
- Insurance is embedded and mandatory for eligible rentals at launch. Non-return or unlawful appropriation is a launch requirement.
- The renter pays the full premium. Do not reintroduce a 70/30 renter/platform split or any 30% OutZila insurance contribution.
- Treat a 10% premium rate and a 10% insurer-paid distribution remuneration as planning hypotheses until written agreements and regulatory validation exist.
- Supabase is for accounts, login and sessions. Signicat is the preferred future civil-identity and signature provider, subject to contracting.

## Production truthfulness

Do not present a pilot or planned integration as a completed legal, financial or insurance operation. The current application must be described accurately as an authenticated operational pilot until real provider integrations and required validation exist.

## Change discipline

- Keep strategy and implementation separate.
- Update the canonical Markdown documents before creating derivative Word files, presentations or external outreach.
- Preserve legacy material for historical reference, but label it clearly and do not let it determine future requirements.
- Do not alter functional code unless the user explicitly requests it.
